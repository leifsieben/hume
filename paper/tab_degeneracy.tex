\caption{Representation degeneracy on 695{,}115 unique biomolecular structures
(the \emph{biostructures} set of Huber and Pollmann~\cite{Huber2026}): compounds sharing a
bit-identical vector with at least one structurally different compound, following their
duplicate test. Every row is computed here on the same compounds with the same code, so the
rates are directly comparable; recomputing their fingerprints this way reproduces each
published rate to within 1.6 points. The right-hand pair splits the collisions by the
largest mass difference within a colliding group---under 1\,Da the pair is an isomer or
tautomer no 2D representation separates, over 400\,Da it is two unrelated molecules given
one vector. HUME is degenerate for no compound at all, down to its 256-column variant,
while the 2{,}048-bit fingerprint it contains is degenerate for 9.07\% with nearly half of
those collisions spanning more than 400\,Da: the descriptors do not merely add signal, they
repair a failure of the fingerprint. Note also that count fingerprints fail benignly where
binary ones do not---every Morgan-3 count collision falls within 1\,Da, against 44.9\%
